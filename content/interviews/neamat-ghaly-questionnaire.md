# Neamat Ghaly — Human Profile Questionnaire

## Introduction for Neamat

Hi Neamat,

We are developing a more personal section for your profile on Miotk Models.

The purpose is to present you not only through photographs, but also through your background, personality, ambitions and the way you think. We want brands and creative teams to understand the person behind the image, while making sure that the final profile still feels natural, elegant and true to you.

Please answer the questions below in your own words. There are no perfect answers, and they do not need to sound formal. Honest and specific responses will work much better than polished “model interview” answers.

You may skip any question that feels too private or that you simply do not want to answer. Your responses will be edited into a shorter profile, and nothing sensitive will be published without your approval.

## Core questions

1. How would you introduce yourself to someone who has never met you, without mentioning your appearance or modeling?

2. Which parts of your background have shaped the person you are today?

3. How has living between Egypt and Poland influenced the way you see yourself?

4. Do you feel that you belong to one culture, both cultures, or somewhere between them?

5. What do people often misunderstand about you when they first meet you?

6. What matters to you more deeply than most people realise?

7. What qualities do you value most in yourself?

8. In what situations do you feel most confident and most like yourself?

9. What kind of person do you want to become over the next few years?

10. What changes in you when you step in front of a camera?

11. What do you bring to a set beyond your appearance?

12. What makes you trust a photographer or creative team?

13. What kind of direction helps you give your best performance during a shoot?

14. What kind of images or campaigns would you genuinely like to create?

15. Is there a side of your personality that has not yet been captured in photographs?

16. What separates a beautiful photograph from a meaningful one?

17. What would you never want your image to communicate?

18. Has your understanding of beauty changed as you have grown older or moved between cultures?

19. What does beauty mean to you when it is separated from social media, trends and other people’s approval?

20. Do you think attractiveness gives people power? If so, what are the limits of that power?

21. What do you think the modeling industry often fails to understand about the people it photographs?

22. How do you feel about AI-generated models and synthetic faces being used in advertising?

23. What can a real human being bring to an image that artificial intelligence cannot reproduce?

24. What kind of work would make you feel proud, rather than simply visible?

25. What would you like brands and creative teams to understand about you before booking you?

26. What are you building for yourself beyond modeling?

27. What does independence mean to you personally?

28. What would you like your life and career to represent five years from now?
